Cliqx has no developer-operated account or backend. The Jellyfin sign-in screen
is an optional client for a media server owned and supplied by the user, so no
Cliqx demo credentials exist and Jellyfin is not required to review the app.

Account-free review path:
1. Complete the short onboarding flow.
2. In Browser, open a public website chosen by the reviewer. Protection status
   and private browsing are available from the browser toolbar and Settings.
3. In Settings, open Local files to exercise the system document picker and
   on-device playback with a reviewer-supplied media file.
4. Settings > About contains the privacy policy and open-source attributions.

"Watch Clean" appears only when a compatible page exposes a playable media
stream. Cliqx does not provide, host, or unlock content.
